What could change the number

  • Bonus level. At the market's 22%, total cash is $280,600: above the cash median, but only 0.94 of market total direct comp, because there is no equity.
  • Scope. Building the function from scratch is what puts the base above the median.
  • No equity. Total direct comp equals total cash, 1.04 of a market that includes equity.
  • Survey choice. Radford leans toward tech but fits the role's analytics skill set; IPAS is within $100 of it on median base.
  • Blending. Good practice blends like-for-like matches, and a hybrid role has no exact one. Broader cuts would lower the number: Radford's comp and benefits family pays $213,200 vs $221,450 median base because it includes benefits roles, so it is left out.
  • Location. US figures. A Canada-based role needs a Canadian cut.
  • Timing. Data is not aged; past six months, add about 3% a year (an assumption).
