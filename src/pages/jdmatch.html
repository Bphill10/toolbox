Job Matcher

Step 1 of building a job structure: load your job descriptions or job titles and each one is matched to a market survey job. Company jobs are already matched below.

Add your jobs

Many files at once, or a zipped folder: job descriptions (Word, PDF) or a list of job titles (Excel, CSV). Each one is matched and priced against the market. Or paste titles below.

Files are read in your browser. Nothing is uploaded or stored.
Job titleEmployeesUS market rangeUS market P50Compa ratioSurvey matchLevel fitConfidence

Market columns use the US survey median for each job; the range is that median ±20%, an assumed range width. The dot is the company’s pay against each employee’s own country market (full-time basis), placed on the US scale. Pay is not shown for jobs with fewer than 5 people; compa ratio is average pay against the market median for each person’s own country. Confidence combines how clearly the description matches one survey job over the next, and how well title, experience and scope agree on a level. Click any row for the reasons and the career path.
