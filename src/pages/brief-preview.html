What could change the number

  1. Bonus level. At the market's 22%, total cash is $280,600: above the cash median, but only 0.94 of market total direct comp, because there is no equity.
  2. Scope. Building the function from scratch is what puts the base above the median.
  3. No equity. Total direct comp equals total cash, 1.04 of a market that includes equity.
  4. Survey choice. Radford leans toward tech but fits the role's analytics skill set; IPAS is within $100 of it on median base.
  5. Blending. Good practice blends like-for-like matches, and a hybrid role has no exact one. Broader cuts would lower the number: Radford's comp and benefits family pays $213,200 vs $221,450 median base because it includes benefits roles, so it is left out.
  6. Location. US figures. A Canada-based role needs a Canadian cut.
  7. Timing. Data is not aged; past six months, add about 3% a year (an assumption).
